## New today (highest momentum first)

### [willfaust/Madeira](https://github.com/willfaust/Madeira) - C - 708 total stars (+171)
- **What it is:** Madeira runs x86-64 Windows PC games on a non-jailbroken iPhone by chaining Wine (ARM64EC), FEX-Emu (x86-64→ARM64 translation), and DXMT (D3D11→Metal) into one process. It's aimed at hobbyist iOS tinkerers rather than mainstream gamers, and has little direct competition since most Windows-game-on-iOS efforts require jailbreaking.
- **Why now: Guess** — dozens of near-identical forks of the repo appeared within days of each other, a pattern consistent with the project being freshly shared and copied across social media/Discord; no single press hit was found.

### [mvschwarz/openrig](https://github.com/mvschwarz/openrig) - TypeScript - 660 total stars (+114)
- **What it is:** OpenRig is a harness that boots and coordinates teams of Claude Code and Codex agent sessions from a single YAML spec, giving them shared topology, messaging, and persistent state via tmux. It competes with ad-hoc multi-terminal agent setups and other emerging "agent team" orchestrators like Paperclip.
- **Why now: Evidence** — a [Show HN post](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47772935) plus a burst of releases, including v0.5.17 shipped today (2026-09-27) and v0.5.15/v0.5.16 on the two days prior.

### [vectorize-io/hindsight](https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight) - Python - 35,636 total stars (+4,463)
- **What it is:** Hindsight is an agent memory system that stores facts, experiences, and beliefs in biomimetic structures and updates them over time, aimed at developers building long-running conversational or autonomous agents. It competes with RAG pipelines and knowledge-graph memory tools.
- **Why now: Evidence** — release v0.10.1 shipped 2026-09-21 (6 days ago), and the launch is backed by an [arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.12818) co-authored with Virginia Tech and The Washington Post plus VentureBeat coverage.

### [debpalash/VoiceStudio](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio) - Python - 38,891 total stars (+3,060)
- **What it is:** VoiceStudio is a fully local voice-cloning, dubbing, transcription, and audiobook suite bundling 16 TTS and 11 ASR engines, aimed at creators who want ElevenLabs-grade output with no account or cloud dependency. It directly targets ElevenLabs as its commercial competitor.
- **Why now: Evidence** — release v0.5.6 shipped 2026-09-23 (4 days ago) adding Twilio phone integration and MCP support, alongside a [dev.to writeup](https://dev.to/terminalchai/voicestudio-a-100-local-open-source-alternative-to-elevenlabs-4bd) and pickup on X.

### [dream-num/univer](https://github.com/dream-num/univer) - TypeScript - 19,930 total stars (+920)
- **What it is:** Univer is an embeddable Office SDK — spreadsheets, docs, slides, and PDFs — built on a shared plugin and canvas-rendering architecture, competing with commercial embed SDKs and libraries like Luckysheet. It's now pitched as "the Office Harness for AI Agents," layering agent-facing APIs on its existing editors.
- **Why now: Guess** — the repo is well-established (already past a 1.0 release), so today's bump most likely reflects its recent repositioning toward AI-agent integration rather than one dated event; no specific announcement was confirmed.

### [paperclipai/paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip) - TypeScript - 88,711 total stars (+2,527)
- **What it is:** Paperclip is an orchestration platform that manages teams of AI agents like company employees, adding org charts, budgets, and governance on top of existing agent frameworks. It competes with lighter-weight orchestrators such as OpenRig by targeting larger, structured "agent workforces."
- **Why now: Evidence** — the project's original [Hacker News launch](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-paperclip-the-ai-agent-orchestration-platform-that-almost-didnt-happen-174e511161) already carried it past 88k stars, it shipped release v2026.916.1 on 2026-09-21 (6 days ago), and it drew security-focused press in August over agent-import command-injection flaws.

### [InfinityLoop1308/PipePipe](https://github.com/InfinityLoop1308/PipePipe) - Shell - 6,420 total stars (+139)
- **What it is:** PipePipe is an Android app forked from NewPipe in 2022 that lets people browse YouTube ad-free with SponsorBlock, dislike counts, and live-chat overlays, run independently of NewPipe's own release cycle. It competes directly with NewPipe and other de-Googled YouTube clients.
- **Why now:** no clear trigger found.

### [vercel-labs/scriptc](https://github.com/vercel-labs/scriptc) - TypeScript - 5,245 total stars (+76)
- **What it is:** scriptc compiles TypeScript straight to native executables, WebAssembly, or C/LLVM IR without bundling a JavaScript engine, letting developers ship small, fast-starting CLIs and servers. It competes with Bun/Deno's compile modes and with Go/Rust for lightweight native binaries.
- **Why now: Evidence** — it's a fresh Vercel Labs project (repo created 2026-07-22) that drew a [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063175) plus coverage from [heise.de](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Vercel-s-scriptc-runs-TypeScript-natively-11388382.html) and InfoQ.

### [rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch](https://github.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch) - Python - 58,840 total stars (+848)
- **What it is:** A 523-lesson, 20-phase curriculum that has learners implement ML/AI algorithms — from backprop to agent loops — by hand before using frameworks, aimed at developers who want to understand AI internals rather than just call APIs. It competes with bootcamp-style courses and framework-first tutorials.
- **Why now: Guess** — the repo is already well past its initial launch (58k+ stars, an associated book series and website), so today's gain looks like continued organic/SEO-driven growth from its creator's other popular projects rather than one specific event.
